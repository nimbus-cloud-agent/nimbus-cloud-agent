# Roadmap

This is a proposed, user-facing roadmap. It describes what users should be
able to do, and why it matters to them — not how any item is implemented.
Items are proposals, not commitments, until they are confirmed.

Ordering principle: simplest and most important first; most complex and
least urgent last.

## 1. One execution mode only

What users can do: use the agent without ever choosing an execution mode.
Why it matters: there is nothing to understand or pick before starting.
Every run has the same safety guarantee, and the product is simpler to use.

## 2. Manage sessions

What users can do: search past sessions, rename them, pin the ones they use
often, export a conversation, and delete sessions they no longer need.
Why it matters: as sessions accumulate, users can find earlier work quickly
instead of scrolling through a long list.

## 3. Reusable task templates

What users can do: save a task they do often as a template, then start it
again with a single action instead of explaining the background from scratch.
Why it matters: it removes repetition for recurring work.

## 4. Long tasks without waiting on the page

What users can do: start a long task, leave, and come back later to a clear
finished state with its results easy to find.
Why it matters: long work does not require keeping the page open and watching
it finish.

## 5. Control a running task

What users can do: stop a task that is going in the wrong direction, retry a
failed turn, and edit a message and rerun it.
Why it matters: one wrong run does not waste the whole session.

## 6. Files in and out

What users can do: send local files to the agent, and download files the
agent creates or changes.
Why it matters: work is not limited to chat text; users can take real
results away with them.

## 7. See what the agent is doing

What users can do: see what the agent is working on now, which files it
changed and how they changed, and why something failed — in a form a person
can read.
Why it matters: users can trust, follow, and intervene in the work instead of
waiting for a final answer in the dark.

## 8. Confirm before irreversible actions

What users can do: get asked first before the agent deletes something,
changes an important setting, or sends a request outside the workspace.
Why it matters: safety is the default, without the user having to configure
it in advance.

## 9. See usage and cost

What users can do: see, for each session, how much was used, how long it
ran, and approximately what it cost.
Why it matters: users are not surprised by usage after the fact.
